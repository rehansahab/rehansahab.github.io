Inside the panel you will find four groups that match your four main options:

  • Gold — type an amount in the box and press the apply button. Your gold count updates the next time you open your character screen.
  • Paragon — turn the switch on and your paragon points are filled to the maximum. Open your paragon board to spend them.
  • Legendary Items — browse the list, tick the items you want, and confirm. They arrive in your inventory.
  • God Mode — flip the single switch on or off at any moment, even during a fight.

Changes apply immediately. There is nothing to save and no restart needed. When you are finished playing, close the trainer window first, then exit the game normally.

🛠️ Troubleshooting the Common Hiccups

The menu does not appear. Make sure Diablo IV is fully loaded and you are standing in the world, not on a loading screen. Then click once on the game window and press the menu key again.

Windows blocks the file. Right-click the trainer file, choose Properties, and look for a checkbox at the bottom labelled "Unblock". Tick it, click Apply, and open the trainer again.

Your antivirus removes the file. Some antivirus tools are very cautious with trainers. Add the "fixi-menu" folder to your antivirus exclusion list, then download the application again from the link at the top of this page.

Nothing happens when you click a button. Close the trainer, close the game completely, open the game again, and reopen the trainer. Always start the game first and the trainer second.

The game updated and the menu stopped working. Open the download page again and grab the newest version of the trainer — updates usually follow game patches within a short time.
